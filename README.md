# village-dog-arg-selection
Trackhub for village dog selection analysis.


Scan for windows with low TMRCA in village dogs relative to wolves

This trackhub contains intervals with an unusually low mean TMRCA in village dogs relative to wolves.

Load the trackhub in the UCSC browser with this link:

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KiddLab/village-dog-arg-selection/main/village-dog-arg-selection.hub.txt


Contact Kidd Lab, University of Michigan for more information

## Track description

The Wolf VD track shows the mean TMRCA for village dogs (in orange) and wolves (in red).

The VD Geo track shows mean TMRCA for each geographically separate village dog group. The
Europe Associated group is in orange, the Africa group in green, Central Asia in blue, and East
and Southeast Asia is in pink.


## How to load the track hub

Go to http://genome.ucsc.edu/

Click on My Data --> Track Hubs

click the 'Connected Hubs' tab
 
paste in this address: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KiddLab/village-dog-arg-selection/main/village-dog-arg-selection.hub.txt

Click 'Add Hub'
